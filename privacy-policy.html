Privacy Policy

Slingshot Smash－Brick Breaker · Effective date: 21 November 2026

  • What we collect: nothing that identifies you. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Selling or sharing: we do not sell or share your data, and the game has no ads.
  • Where it's stored: your progress and settings stay in a save file on your device.
  • Deleting it: uninstall the game or clear its storage. More on deletion.

1. Who we are

Slingshot Smash－Brick Breaker (the "Game") is made by NadeSoft, Bangladesh ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy, email contact@nadesoft.bd.

2. What the Game stores

The Game keeps a small save file so you can carry on where you left off. It never leaves your device through us.

DataExamplesWhy
Game progressUnlocked levels, last level played, stars and best score per levelTo keep your progress between sessions
SettingsMusic, Sound and Vibration on or offTo remember your choices
Hints seenWhether you have already seen the aiming tip and each new-ball tipSo the same tip isn't shown twice

Coins and gems you collect in a level are a celebration for that attempt only and are not saved.

3. What we don't collect

The Game has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics or tracking SDK, and no leaderboards or chat. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone. It does not use the advertising ID.

4. Services outside our control

Google Play

Google handles downloads, updates and, if you have opted in on your device, crash and performance reports that it shares with developers in anonymous, aggregate form (Android vitals). Google processes this under the Google Privacy Policy.

Android backup

If backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include the Game's save file in your device backup to your Google Account and restore it on a new phone. Google runs this backup; we cannot see or access it.

Unity engine

The Game is built with the Unity engine. Unity may receive basic, non-identifying technical information when the Game starts, such as device model, operating system version, graphics hardware and screen size, which Unity uses to improve its engine. This is covered by the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. We do not receive any information that identifies you from it.

5. How we use data and our legal basis

The save file is used only to run the Game for you, which is needed to provide the service you asked for (contract, under GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Any technical information received by Unity or Google is processed by them under their own legal bases and policies.

6. Who we share data with

We don't hold any of your personal data, so we have nothing to share or sell. If a future version adds anything that changes this, such as ads or cloud saves, we will update this policy first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent in the Game.

7. How long data is kept

The save file stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its storage. A copy in your Android backup is kept for as long as Google's backup settings keep it.

8. Your choices and rights

You control the save file: you can reset it by clearing the Game's storage, or remove it by uninstalling. Music, Sound and Vibration can be changed at any time under Settings on the home screen or from the Paused menu.

If you live in the EU, UK or another region with data protection laws (such as the GDPR, UK GDPR or the California CCPA), you have the right to access, correct, delete or export personal data and to object to its use. Because we don't hold any personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to return or delete, but you can still email contact@nadesoft.bd and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Deleting your data

There is no account to delete. To remove everything the Game stores, uninstall it, or go to Settings → Apps → Slingshot Smash－Brick Breaker → Storage → Clear storage on your device (labels vary slightly by phone). Full steps are on the data deletion page.

10. Security

The save file is kept in the Game's own app storage on your device, which other apps cannot normally read. The Game does not send your progress anywhere, so there is no server copy to protect.

11. Children

The Game is suitable for all ages. It does not ask for or knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). It has no ads, chat or purchases. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

12. This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, trackers or external fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log your IP address for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change affects what the Game collects, we will also mention it in the update notes on Google Play.
