Delete your data

Slingshot Smash－Brick Breaker · Developer: NadeSoft

Slingshot Smash has no accounts. You never sign up or sign in, and we keep no data about you on any server. Everything the game stores is in a save file on your own device, and you can delete it at any time.

What the game stores

  • Game progress: unlocked levels, stars and best scores
  • Settings: Music, Sound and Vibration
  • Which first-time tips you have already seen

How to delete it

Option 1: Clear the game's storage (keeps the game installed)

  1. Open your device's Settings.
  2. Tap Apps (on some phones, Apps & notifications → See all apps).
  3. Find and tap Slingshot Smash－Brick Breaker.
  4. Tap Storage & cache (or Storage), then Clear storage and confirm.

The next time you open the game, it starts from level 1 with default settings.

Option 2: Uninstall the game

Press and hold the game icon and choose Uninstall, or uninstall it from the Google Play app. Android deletes the save file with the game.

Android backup

If device backup is on, Android may keep a copy of the save file in your Google Account backup. You can manage or delete backups in your device's Settings → Google → Backup, or at drive.google.com under Storage → Backups. Google controls this backup; we have no access to it.

Request by email

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing on our side to delete. If you have emailed us and want that conversation deleted, write from the same address to contact@nadesoft.bd with the subject "Data deletion request - Slingshot Smash". We will delete the emails within 14 days and confirm by reply.

What is kept

Nothing is kept by NadeSoft. Anonymous, aggregate statistics that Google Play shows developers (such as install counts and crash rates) cannot be linked to you and are not affected by deletion.
