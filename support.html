Frequently asked questions

Playing

How do I aim and shoot?

Put your finger on the screen and drag down to pull the slingshot back. Drag further for a stronger, higher shot, and drag left or right to steer. The dotted line shows where the ball will go. Let go to fire, or slide back up before letting go to cancel.

How do the special balls work?

Each level gives you a set of balls, shown at the bottom of the screen. Heavy smashes straight through, Explosive, Ice and Splitter do something special when you tap the screen while the ball is in flight. A short tip appears the first time you get each new ball.

How do I get three stars?

Stars depend on your score. You earn points for every brick you knock loose or smash, and when you clear a level each ball you didn't need adds a 1,000-point bonus. So bring down as much as you can with as few balls as possible. Your best score for each level is saved.

I ran out of balls. What now?

Tap Retry to play the level again. Try hitting lower down, where the building is supported, so more of it falls at once.

How do I unlock new maps?

Finish the levels in order. Completing a level unlocks the next one, and finishing a map's last level opens the next map: Meadow Village, Harbor Town, Desert Fort, Frost Peaks and Sky Citadel.

What happens to the coins and gems I collect?

They are part of the fun of smashing a level and count towards your score for that attempt. They are not a saved balance and can't be spent or bought.

Sound and settings

How do I turn off the music, sound or vibration?

Tap the Settings button on the home screen, or the pause button during a level to open Paused, then use the Music, Sound and Vibration buttons.

The game feels slow on my phone.

Close other apps, turn off battery saver if it's on and restart the game. The game lowers its effects on older devices automatically. If it's still slow, email us your device model.

Progress and devices

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. There's no sign-in, and the game plays fully offline.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Progress is saved on your device. If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, it may be restored when you set up a new phone with the same Google Account. Otherwise a new install starts from level 1.

I reinstalled the game and lost my progress.

Uninstalling removes the save file from the device. We don't keep a copy on a server, so we can't restore it, but an Android backup may bring it back on a fresh install.

How do I reset my progress or delete my data?

Clear the game's storage in your device's settings, or uninstall it. Step-by-step instructions are on the data deletion page.

Privacy

Does the game have ads or in-app purchases?

No. The game is free with no ads and nothing to buy.

What data does the game collect?

None that identifies you. See the Privacy Policy for the details.
